## 🎚️ 4. Audio, Music & Production Recommendations

1. **Background Music:**
   - Cinematic ambient synthesizer tracks with steady, subtle pulse (similar to Hans Zimmer’s *Interstellar* or Max Richter).
   - Recommended Tempo: 60–70 BPM.
   - Volume: Set at -18dB to -20dB (approx. 12–15% volume) so the spoken narration stands out crisply.
2. **Narration Audio:**
   - Clean cardioid microphone or dynamic mic with pop filter.
   - Pacing: 118 words per minute. Do not rush; allow the 1-second pauses after key hooks to breathe.
3. **Visual Transitions:**
   - Rapid cuts or 0.2-second cross-dissolves between views.
   - Screen Recording Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p) at 60 FPS in fullscreen mode (`F11`).
