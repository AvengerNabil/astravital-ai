## 🌌 Overview

**AstraVital AI** is an advanced, full-stack astronaut health intelligence platform designed for long-duration human exploration missions, including the **Artemis Lunar Base** and **Ares IV Mars Expeditions**.

This upgrade enriches the platform with authentic, peer-reviewed NASA research datasets from the **NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR)**, **NASA GeneLab**, and the **NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS)**, establishing rigorous scientific provenance while preserving every existing feature of the original platform.

The system bridges clinical terrestrial medicine, spaceflight biology, high-frequency simulated astronaut telemetry, interactive 3D celestial orbital environments, and an interactive 3D Astronaut Digital Twin.

## 🔬 Authentic NASA Research Data Integration

Official portals: [osdr.nasa.gov](https://osdr.nasa.gov/) | [genelab.nasa.gov](https://genelab.nasa.gov/)

The platform integrates authentic spaceflight biology studies and metadata, linking them directly to corresponding astronaut health indicators:
1. **OSD-379 / GLDS-379**: Multi-omics blood and urine profiling across ISS Expeditions 58–63 (mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation downregulation and cytokine elevation). *Linked to Stress, Core Temp, Heart Rate.*
2. **OSD-205 / GLDS-205**: NASA Twins Study longitudinal profiling of twin astronauts Scott and Mark Kelly (telomere length dynamics, carotid artery stiffness, cognitive latency). *Linked to Heart Rate, Blood Pressure, Cognitive Performance.*
3. **OSD-120 / GLDS-120**: Spaceflight soleus muscle atrophy and transcriptomic remodeling in microgravity (MuRF1 / MAFbx ubiquitin ligase activation). *Linked to Physical Exercise, Muscle Cross-Sectional Area.*
4. **OSD-665 / GLDS-665**: Simulated Galactic Cosmic Radiation (GCR) induced endothelial cell senescence and microvascular permeability. *Linked to Radiation Dose Rate, Cardiovascular Remodeling.*
5. **OSD-563 / GLDS-563**: Microgravity-induced osteoclast hyperactivation and trabecular bone demineralization (distal femur trabecular loss). *Linked to Bone Density, Physical Exercise.*
6. **OSD-473 / GLDS-473**: Cephalad venous engorgement and internal jugular vein retrograde flow (SANS cohort). *Linked to Blood Pressure, Cephalic Fluid Shift.*
7. **OSD-365 / GLDS-365**: Circadian desynchronization and REM sleep fragmentation in LEO (urinary 6-sulfatoxymelatonin suppression). *Linked to Sleep Duration, Autonomic Stress Score.*
8. **OSD-612 / GLDS-612**: Spaceflight intravascular plasma volume contraction and Gauer-Henry reflex natriuresis. *Linked to Hydration Level, Orthostatic Intolerance.*

## 📡 Simulated Astronaut Telemetry Engine

To ensure operational realism when live classified astronaut biosensors are unavailable, AstraVital AI incorporates a high-fidelity synthetic telemetry engine generating continuous time-series across all **10 required parameters**:
1. **Heart Rate (BPM)**
2. **Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO2 %)**
3. **Body Temperature (°C)**
4. **Blood Pressure (Systolic / Diastolic mmHg)**
5. **Respiratory Rate (breaths/min)**
6. **Sleep Duration (Hours / 24h)**
7. **Physical Activity & Exercise Counters (Reps, Duration, Calories, METs)**
8. **Autonomic Stress Score (0–100)**
9. **Ionizing Radiation Rate (µSv/h)**
10. **Hydration Indicator (% Intravascular Volume)**

### Mandatory Labeling Standard
Every simulated metric visibly displays:
`"SIMULATED DATA – NOT REAL ASTRONAUT TELEMETRY"`

### Simulation Engine Features
- **Deterministic Reproducibility**: Uses configurable seeds (e.g. `seed: 1337`) for exact scientific repeatability.
- **Scenario Injector**:
  - `Nominal Baseline`: Standard healthy astronaut microgravity adaptation.
  - `Warning (Autonomic Stress)`: Sinus tachycardia (96 bpm), mild hypoxia (94.2%), and elevated stress.
  - `Critical (Solar Particle Event & Hypoxia)`: Severe radiation surge (118 µSv/h), tachycardia (124 bpm), and desaturation.
- **Interactive Controls**: Play, Pause, Resume, Reset, and Configurable Update Interval.
- **Data Export**: One-click download of telemetry history in **JSON** and **CSV** formats, with full disclaimer headers and timestamps.
- **Physical Exercise Simulator**: Integrated user workout tracking module simulating repetitions, active duration, target heart rates, and caloric burn.
